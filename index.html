Patient-owned referrals for PMOS

The referral agent that gets a PMOS patient from her PCP to the right OB/GYN, and back.

PMOS is the new name for PCOS. Attune turns a patient's tracked symptoms, portal notes and labs into one referral packet. It finds an in-network OB/GYN with an open time, gets the PCP office to sign off, sends the packet the way each office already receives referrals, and follows up until the visit happens. A person approves every step.

Where referrals break today

From 49 discovery interviews with patients, PCP staff, specialist intake staff and OB/GYNs. The patient is the only one who sees the whole handoff.

  • PCP office

    The medical assistant pulls notes and labs from separate systems and faxes them by hand. Finding an in-network specialist with a working fax number takes the longest. After the fax, nobody knows what happened.

  • Specialist office

    Referrals arrive as faxes in the EHR inbox with partial records. Schedulers call the patient, and the provider retakes the history even when notes were sent.

  • Patient

    She bounces between providers, chases the referral herself and retells her history from memory. Some skip the referral entirely because of insurance or the wait.

For patients

Book a specialist the way you book any appointment. Your PCP signs off behind the scenes.

  • Pick a doctor and a time. In-network OB/GYNs with real openings, distance and PMOS experience on each card. Tap a time to hold it.
  • Choose what's shared. Tracker summary, photo trend, portal notes, labs, uploads. Preview the exact packet the doctor gets.
  • No chasing. Your PCP office signs, Attune sends it with a receipt, and the office confirms your held time. You see each step as it happens.
  • Stop sharing any time. Before it's sent, the request is pulled back. After, nothing further is shared.

For PCP offices

A referral that arrives pre-filled. Check it, sign once, and see what happens after the fax.

  • Pre-filled packet. Reason, the patient-approved records with their sources, and draft ICD-10 symptom codes for the MA to confirm.
  • No specialist hunting. The patient's chosen in-network OB/GYN, with a verified fax or Direct address and NPI.
  • Sign once. The MA signs. A physician co-signs when the plan requires it (HMO and POS by default). Attune never signs.
  • Status after sending. Received, booked and seen come back to you. Export the packet as a PDF into Tebra or any EMR. Web, no install.

For specialist offices

New patients who arrive pre-qualified, with a time already requested.

  • Pre-qualified. Plan and network checked, PCP signed, packet attached before it reaches your inbox.
  • Evidence with sources. Cycle history, hirsutism trend and labs on one page, each item labelled with where it came from. Decision support, not a diagnosis.
  • Confirm in one click. Confirm the patient's requested time, offer another, ask for more records, or decline with a note.
  • Fits your intake. Arrives by verified e-fax or Direct (360X), so it still lands in athena or Epic. Cancellation fill offers an open slot to the next referred patient whose record is ready.

Rules Attune follows

These are enforced in the referral rules and covered by tests, not just written down.

  • The patient approves every share.Nothing leaves Attune until she approves what goes and to whom.
  • Nothing is sent before the PCP office signs.The MA signs every referral, and a physician signs where the plan requires it. The agent never signs.
  • Matches are never ranked by who pays.Ranking uses network, soonest opening, PMOS experience and distance only, and includes specialists who don't work with Attune.
  • Decision support, never a diagnosis.Evidence is mapped to criteria with its source. Codes are drafts until the PCP office confirms them.

Where Attune is today

Attune is an early demo with synthetic data. Nothing in it is sent to a real office. The first loop is PCP to OB/GYN in Dallas–Fort Worth; endocrinology and fertility referrals come later. Attune is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
