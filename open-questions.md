# Open questions

Most of the model rests on one PCP practice, one MA, one specialist MA and a handful of gynos. Tick a box when it's answered and link the note.

## For the team
- [ ] Is the agent part of Plus, or a separate add-on? Does Base get a free first referral?
- [ ] Which number goes in the deck: $10 to $20 per referral, $40 per booking, or no per-patient fee for now? (ADR-004 leans to none.)
- [ ] Do we name the 3 to 5 DFW OB/GYNs and 3 PCPs for a pilot, and which EHRs do they run?
- [ ] Does the agent ever recommend an endo first, or always gyno first, then endo or REI by rule?
- [ ] Who owns HIPAA, BAA and FDA questions?

## For PCP MAs and office managers
- [ ] Would you trust a packet you didn't assemble? What would you check before signing?
- [ ] How long does one referral take today, from start to fax confirmation?
- [ ] How many OB/GYN referrals are for cycle, hormone or PMOS-type symptoms? (PCP-1 offered to look.)
- [ ] Which plans in your patient mix need a referral (HMO, POS) and which don't?
- [ ] Would status updates after sending (received, booked, seen) change anything for you or the doctor?
- [ ] Who decides on new tools, and does anything get bought outside the EMR?

## For specialist intake staff
- [ ] What happens to a faxed referral from arrival to first call? Where does it stall?
- [ ] What's missing most often in a referral packet?
- [ ] How do you fill a cancellation today, and how often do slots go empty?
- [ ] Would you accept a referral in a web inbox, or does it have to land in Athena or Epic? Do you have a Direct address?

## For OB/GYNs and endos
- [ ] Which 5 data points would make a first PMOS visit faster? Which would you ignore?
- [ ] Would you trust patient-logged data if the source of each item was shown?
- [ ] Is cancellation fill worth a monthly fee? At what price, and who signs?
- [ ] When you refer a PMOS patient to an endo or REI, what do you send and what comes back?

## For patients
- [ ] Would you let an app pull your portal records and labs to build a referral? What would stop you?
- [ ] Did you ever skip or lose a referral? Why (insurance, wait, no call back)?
- [ ] Would you pay for an agent that gets you seen sooner? How much, once or monthly?
- [ ] Did you go through a PCP or straight to a gyno?

## For advisors
- [ ] Attorney: can any specialist-paid model work under AO 26-15 and Texas 102.001? What structure is safest?
- [ ] Advisor (ex-Epic): is a SMART on FHIR read app realistic for small practices? Cheapest path to write-back? Is 360X live at DFW Epic sites?
- [ ] Regulatory: does mapping data to Rotterdam criteria count as clinical decision support that needs FDA clearance?
- [ ] Clinical: confirm the mFG threshold (we use 6, per the 2023 guideline's 4 to 6 range) and the symptom-code approach.
